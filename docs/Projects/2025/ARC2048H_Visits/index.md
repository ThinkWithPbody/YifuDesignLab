- Why adaptive re-use?
    - 90% of real estate growth within the next 10 years will entail the adaptive reuse of existing buildings rather than new buildings
    - UofT and TMU have essentially halted all new construction, will be focusing almost exclusively on adaptive reuse projects for the next 8-10 years
    - OPPORTUNITIES
        - Environmental Benefits
            - Lowered carbon footprints
            - Conservation of resources
            - more sustainable developments
        - Economic Benefits
            - ﻿﻿Cost savings (financial incentives in some cases)
            - ﻿﻿Urban revitalization
            - ﻿﻿Increased property values
        - Social / Cultural Benefits
            - Preservation of community identity
            - ing + public space opportunities creation
            - Heritage preservation - inherent cultural value
        - Regulatory Barriers
            - Potential zoning challenge elated to change of use
            - Updates require bringing older buildings up to current codes
        - Less Flexibility
            - Working with existing structural systems - floor loads and bay sizes
            - Navigating more complex design solutions
            - Navigating more complex code solutions
        - Financial Constraints & Risk
            - Developers must use a purchase and finance model, rather than pro-rated investments associated with new-builds
            - Updating older buildings will bring unforeseen costs and complexity
            - Challenges in securing insurance and underwriting
            - Professional liability
            - Required specialty trades can be costly
    - Architectural Restoration vs Restorative Architecture
        - Reduced waste
