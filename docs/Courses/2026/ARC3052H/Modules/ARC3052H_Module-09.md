### Module-09 Money, Power, and Pay: Firm Finances + Architecture Union (FARE Wage)

- Business
- Ownership Structure
    - A practice will start simple, such as sole proprietorship
    - More complex could be partnership, corporations or partnership of corporations
        - OAA registrar issue a certificate of practice to members in partnership
        - In corporations, non-architects owning shares must be full-time employeses
            - non-architects must not own more than 40%
    - Affected by professional relationship, tax implications, exposure of personal assets
    - Perkins & Will chain like ownership
    - Diamond & Schmitt corporation
- Cashflow management
    - Cash important
        - Check might bounce
        - Tracking how much money goes in and out helps you predict how much money will be available in the future
- Profit
- Balance sheet
- Billing rate calculation
    - Employee $40/h
    - Payroll burden $10
    - Sub-total $50
    - Overhead $50
    - Profit $25 (Half of overhead)
    - Billing rate $125/h
    - RFP is like a contract 1, currency is hours
        - Be careful when you bill competitively because you might get a lot of work
        - With municipalities almost always goes to the lowest bidder - public money
        - With other projects think about where to position your service - Brigitte Shim
        - Currently, profession is suffocated with competitive bids
        - Once you get the job it’s yours and you do what you want with it!
