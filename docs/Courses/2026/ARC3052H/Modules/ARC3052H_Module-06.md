### Module-06 When Things Go Wrong: Risk Mitigation + Professional Liability Insurance + Contract Administration

Standard of care
- determined through
	- Expert advice
	- Rules of professional conduct
	- Practice bulletins and advisories
	- Time and place of the professional services
	- Adherence to building codes and regulations
		- Objectives based
		- An alternate solution may be accepted
	- Representation of special skill, historic buildings?
- Exposure to liability
	- Breach of contract
		- Easier to establish breach of contract which is black and white than negligence which is subject to interpretation
		- Must prove all 3
			- A breach of a term of the contract, and
			- Loss or damage caused by the breach, and
			- Damages suffered are within limits permitted by law
	- Negligence or “in Tort”
		- Must prove all 4
			- …
		- Dity of care
			- To the public
				- Slipping pedestrian can sue architect for medical expenses and income
			- To the contractor
				- Constructable using normally available techniques, or else duty to warn
	- Breach of contract or in Tort?
		- Volvo owner don’ have a contract with anyone, but negatively affected by owner or architect or engineer or contractor or anyone that shares responsibility for that cladding
